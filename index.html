A morning reading

This place. This person. This day.

comi begins from the life you are actually in. Your birth chart, the character of the day, and the place where you are standing, read together before the day gets loud.

  1. 01

    The person

    Your date, time, and city of birth become a chart comi keeps. Each day is read against that chart, so the guidance follows you.

  2. 02

    The day

    Each morning offers a direction to face, a measure of the day’s momentum, and a few words for how to meet it. You can begin here. A room scan can come later, when you want the building in the reading.

  3. 03

    The place

    A home, an office, or a site you are considering. Set which way it faces. comi sets that facing beside your chart and the year you are in, so the place and the person are held in the same view.

Classical guidance, held lightly, for the day in front of you.
